# Demo runbook

1. Open [the completed case](https://patch3-q.vercel.app/#missed-deadline). The program accepted and reviewed the report, then missed its final decision deadline.
2. Wait for **Four receipts confirmed on Diana**. Open **View escalation** and **View payout**. The researcher and two auditors authorized payment of 2,500 testnet tUSDT to the committed address.
3. Select **Try signing in your browser**. Choose **Reporter after escalation**, then create and verify the quorum approval. The signing exercise uses synthetic data and sends no transaction.
4. To verify all five completed V5 cases without keys, run `pnpm verify:evidence:v5`.

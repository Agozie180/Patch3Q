# QoreChain customer demonstration

Patch3Q is framed around a real published intake route: QoreChain's security page at
`https://qorechain.io/security` lists `security@qore.network`. The app includes a **Load QoreChain synthetic customer
scenario** action that fills a clearly labelled, fictional duplicate-signer report addressed to that program.

This is honest customer evidence:

- the organization and published security contact are real;
- the vulnerability details are synthetic and explicitly say they are not a live QoreChain finding;
- no email or vulnerability report has been sent to QoreChain;
- no claim is made that QoreChain is a customer, partner, or user of Patch3Q.

The intended public demo replaces mailbox custody with a locally sealed bundle, two independent decryption paths,
an on-chain first-report receipt, deadline escalation, and a two-party post-quantum payout decision. Contacting the
security address should happen only with a harmless demonstration link or with a genuine report that follows the
published policy.
